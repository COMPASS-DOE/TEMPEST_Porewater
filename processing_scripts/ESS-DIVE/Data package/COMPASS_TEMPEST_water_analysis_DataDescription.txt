These data are measurements of porewater and source water samples collected in 2022-2025 from the COMPASS TEMPEST site in the Chesapeake Bay region in three experimental plots: Saltwater Treatment (S), Freshwater Treatment (F), and Control (C). Porewater was collected from lysimeters at 15, 30, and 50 cm depths semi-monthly from 2022-2025, and 1-2 times per day during flooding events. Porewater samples were pulled from lysimeters using an extraction kit consisting of a syringe and tubing, and source water samples were collected from a barge on the nearby estuary (estuary source water samples) or from the emitter lines (F and S source water samples). These samples were then aliquoted into bottles for analysis of sulfate, chloride, sulfide, nutrient, and dissolved organic carbon concentrations. Samples were analyzed for pH, conductivity, and temperature at the time of sample collection. Analysis of each constituent is described in the data dictionary and further details, including quality assurance and quality control for all analyses can be found on the COMPASS TEMPEST Porewater page (https://github.com/COMPASS-DOE/TEMPEST_Porewater)
